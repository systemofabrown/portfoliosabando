Yavapai-Apache Cultural Center

Dennis Numkena's Yavapai-Apache Cultural Center stood at the Montezuma Castle interchange near Camp Verde, Arizona, until it was lost to fire. Our team reconstructed it from what survives and tells its story through the people who rode, walked and gathered there.

  • A film walkthrough of the drawing set, played straight through or stepped drawing by drawing
  • A live exploded 3D model with labeled parts and renders of each space
  • The story behind the building, and photographs of the physical model
